# Recording guide for Ticha's reading sounds

## How to record (easiest way — no technical steps for you)

1. Open your phone's Voice Memos app (or any recorder).
2. Press record, then read down a batch below, saying each item **once**, clearly and naturally, with about a 1-second pause before the next one. Don't say the batch number or file names out loud — just the sounds/words, in order.
3. If you mess one up, just pause, say "again", then repeat it correctly — I'll cut out the mistake when I split the file.
4. Save/export each batch as its own audio file (any format is fine — m4a, mp3, wav, whatever Voice Memos gives you).
5. Send me all the batch files. I'll split each one into the separate named files the app needs and drop them into place.

Do Batch 1 first — it's the most important (vowels, alone). Everything after that can happen over the next few days, in any order, whenever you have 10–15 minutes.

**A note on "ng'"** (as in ng'ombe): say it as its own nasal sound, not "ng" + apostrophe spelled out.

**Style:** record each single letter/syllable the way you'd teach a child — calm, clear, a little slower than normal speech. Words can be said naturally.
